💡 Execution Tips for Manual Arb

⏱ Speed matters
At $100–500 scale, you have a wider window than bots since your price impact is tiny. Aim to execute both legs within 60 seconds of spotting the opp.
✅ Set slippage correctly
Use 0.5% slippage on both DEXes. Too low and your tx will revert. Too high and you give back profit. PulseX: Settings → Slippage Tolerance.
📊 Target 0.6%+ spreads
Two swaps cost ~0.58% in fees. You need a gross spread above that to profit. Spreads above 1% are much safer after slippage. Ignore "Slim" signals.
🔄 Buy leg first
Always complete the buy leg first (cheaper DEX). Then immediately execute the sell leg. Never wait between legs — prices move.
